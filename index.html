IoT-Based Medication Monitoring System

SMART MEDICINE BOX

Overview

An IoT-enabled medicine reminder system utilizing ESP32-CAM, buzzer, LED, and LCD alerts. Implemented Python email automation for missed doses and integrated a Telegram Bot API to capture and send intake confirmation images.

The Challenge

Missing medication doses can lead to severe health consequences for patients, particularly the elderly or those with chronic conditions who have complex daily medication schedules.

The Solution

A smart, connected hardware device that automatically reminds patients to take their medicine and verifies intake. It provides peace of mind to caregivers through remote alerts and visual confirmation.

How It Works

Key Features

  • Remote email automation for missed doses
